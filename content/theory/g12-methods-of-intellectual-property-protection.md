# Problems of disseminating and using information
@lo 12.1.3.4

:::definition Intellectual property (IP)
Creations of the mind — software, music, films, books, designs, inventions, logos — that belong to their creators and are protected by law (**copyright**, **patents**, **trademarks**).
:::

:::compare Problems
| Problem | Description | Consequences |
|---|---|---|
| Software piracy | Copying, distributing or using software without a licence | Loss of income for developers; legal fines; pirated copies often contain malware |
| Copyright infringement | Using music, images, video or text without permission | Removal of content, lawsuits, fines |
| Plagiarism | Presenting someone else's work as your own | Academic penalties; loss of reputation |
| Misinformation and fake news | False information spreads quickly on social media | Panic, wrong decisions, damage to reputations |
| Deepfakes | AI-generated fake video/audio of real people | Fraud, blackmail, political manipulation |
| Leaks of confidential information | Publishing private or secret documents | Harm to people and organisations, legal action |
| Unreliable sources | Using information without checking its accuracy | Errors in work and decisions |
:::

:::compare Ways to protect intellectual property
| Method | How it protects |
|---|---|
| Copyright © | Automatic protection of original works; gives the author exclusive rights to copy and distribute |
| Licences (EULA, Creative Commons, GPL) | Define exactly how others may use the work |
| Patents | Protect inventions for a fixed period (registered) |
| Trademarks ™ ® | Protect names and logos |
| Technical measures | Product keys, activation, DRM, watermarks, encryption |
:::

:::tip
To use information legally: check the **licence**, **ask permission**, **cite the source** (author, title, date, URL) and use **open-licence** materials (Creative Commons, public domain).
:::

# Using e-government resources
@lo 12.1.3.7

:::definition E-government
Providing **government services online**. In Kazakhstan the main portal is **eGov.kz**, with mobile access through the **eGov mobile** app.
:::

:::compare Examples of services
| Service | Example |
|---|---|
| Certificates | Certificate of address registration, no criminal record |
| Education | Enrolling a child in school or kindergarten, applying for grants |
| Taxes and payments | Paying taxes, fines and utility bills |
| Documents | Applying for an ID card, passport or driving licence |
| Health | Registering with a clinic, booking a doctor's appointment |
:::

:::steps Signing in securely
- Sign in with your **IIN** and a password, an **EDS** (electronic digital signature) key, or a one-time code / biometrics in the mobile app.
- Keep the EDS key file and its password **private** — it is your legal signature.
- Check the website address (`egov.kz`) and HTTPS padlock to avoid **phishing** copies.
- Log out on shared computers.
:::

:::compare Advantages and disadvantages of e-government
| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Services available 24/7 from anywhere | Needs Internet access and digital skills |
| No queues; saves time and travel | Risk of phishing and identity theft |
| Less paperwork and corruption; transparent | System failures make services unavailable |
| Faster processing | Concerns about the security of personal data |
:::
